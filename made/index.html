  • Headboard

    December 6, 2025 · furniture

    Kevin designed and built this bed for us. I created a headboard with foam and canvas. The little buttons were 3D printed and wrapped with denim. It was fun to staple fabric - so much easier than sewing and completely finished object. The design didn’t work out because the panel left a gap below that provided the opposite of back support. Learned: A headboard really needs to be a flat surface all the way down behind the mattress edge.

  • Coat rack

    November 26, 2025 · 3Dprinting

    I wanted a row of hooks for the entryway so I asked Kevin to cut me a piece of wood for the backing. I finished it and added hooks that I designed and printed. It’s holding up well! It was a real pain to install though. The mounting screws are behind two of the hooks. It was really hard to line up the mounting screws with the wall holes because the board blocked my view. In the future, I’d prefer to screw a mount into the wall and then slide this down over the mount, like how pictures are hung.

  • Refurbished Shelf

    November 7, 2025 · refurbishing

    I found this Ikea shelf on the road. It had been painted over with an ugly brown. Now it looks like the original. This was my first project to refinish wood furniture and I decided it’s not for me. Those little dowels between the side supports could not be reached with the orbital sander and I didn’t like doing that by hand. =p The finish is a white Oslo.
